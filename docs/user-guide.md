# CoFoundry User Guide

## Welcome to CoFoundry

CoFoundry is a social media platform for startups and founders to build in public, share milestones, and connect with investors. Our platform is designed to create a transparent, community-first space where stakeholders in the startup ecosystem can connect, collaborate, and grow publicly.

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Visit the CoFoundry platform and click on "Sign Up"
2. Fill in your details:
   - Username
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Display name
   - Account type (Founder, Startup, or Investor)
3. Click "Create Account" to complete registration

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click "Login"

### Navigation

- **Home Feed**: View posts from your network and discover new content
- **Discover**: Explore trending topics, founders, and startups
- **Profile**: View and edit your profile information
- **Notifications**: Check updates and interactions with your content

## Core Features

### Build In Public

The #BuildInPublic movement is central to CoFoundry. Share your:

- Daily progress
- Challenges and solutions
- Learning experiences
- Metric updates
- Launch preparations

### Milestone Tracking

- Create milestone posts to highlight significant achievements
- Set future milestone targets
- Track progress toward goals
- Celebrate wins with the community

### SOS Flares

When you need urgent help:

1. Create a post with the "SOS" type
2. Set your "Pressure Gauge" level (1-10)
3. Describe the specific challenge or question
4. Receive targeted assistance from the community

### Live Streaming

Share real-time updates:

1. Click "Go Live" in the post creation area
2. Grant camera/microphone permissions
3. Add a title and description
4. Start sharing with your audience

## Content Creation

### Standard Posts

1. Navigate to the home page
2. Click in the "What's happening?" box
3. Type your update
4. Add media (images, videos) if desired
5. Click "Post" to publish

### Milestone Posts

1. Click "Create Post"
2. Select "Milestone" post type
3. Add milestone title and date
4. Describe the achievement or goal
5. Add relevant media
6. Click "Post Milestone"

### Launch Announcements

1. Click "Create Post"
2. Select "Launch" post type
3. Add launch title and URL
4. Describe what you're launching
5. Add screenshots or demo videos
6. Click "Announce Launch"

## Fundraising & Financials

### Creating a Fundraising Campaign

1. Navigate to your profile
2. Go to the "Fundraising" tab
3. Click "Create Campaign"
4. Set your funding goal and timeline
5. Describe what you're raising funds for
6. Add pitch materials and financials
7. Launch your campaign

### Financial Information

Track and share:

- Capital Expenditures
- Revenue Streams
- Operational Expenses
- Financial projections

## FAQ

### How do I upgrade to Pro?

Visit your profile and click on the "Upgrade to Pro" banner to see available plans and complete your subscription.

### Can I change my account type?

Account types cannot be changed after registration. If needed, create a new account with the desired type.

### How do I contact support?

Email support@cofoundry.com for assistance or use the help center accessible via the settings menu.

### Is my financial information public?

Financial information is only visible to those you choose to share it with. Set visibility controls in the financials section.

### How do I report inappropriate content?

Click the flag icon on any post or comment to report it to our moderation team for review.
